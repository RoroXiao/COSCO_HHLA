对 COSCO 为什么重要 WHY IT MATTERS · TO COSCO

  • 航线安全：欧洲网络的北翼节点——Piraeus 67%、Zeebrugge ~100% 之后的汉堡少数股权Route security: the northern anchor of our European network — a minority stake in Hamburg after Piraeus (67%) and Zeebrugge (~100%).
  • 客户变股东：35 年汉堡根基（1989 年起欧洲总部），1/3 集装箱与中国相关——投资是既有业务的深化，不是新进入Customer turned shareholder: 35 years of Hamburg roots (European HQ since 1989), 1 in 3 containers China-related — a deepening of existing business, not a new entry.
  • 长期财务利益：股权=对码头长期绩效的 stake，与一次性运输合同不同Long-term financial interest: equity is a stake in the terminal's long-term performance — unlike a one-off shipping contract.

对汉堡与德国为什么重要 WHY IT MATTERS · TO HAMBURG & GERMANY

  • 锚定客户：吞吐量保障——2025 年 8.3m TEU +7.3%、中国贸易 +6.5%，增长已回来且中国是最大伙伴Anchor customer and volume security — 2025: 8.3m TEU (+7.3%), China trade +6.5%: growth is back, and China is the largest partner.
  • 抗周期资本：不因周期转向而撤离的股东；对 HHLA（5,600+ 员工、上市公司）是资本与货量的双重支撑Counter-cyclical capital: a shareholder that stays when the cycle turns — capital and cargo for HHLA (5,600+ employees, listed company).
  • 德国最大贸易伙伴：中国连续六年第一（占外贸 9.5%）——依赖是双向的China has been Germany's largest trading partner for six years running (~9.5% of foreign trade) — the dependence runs both ways.

诚意记录 SINCERITY ON THE RECORD — 六个可核查的信号 · six verifiable signals

  • ① 接受降比例：35% → 24.99%，低于 25% 否决权门槛——德国法律写下的边界，我们签在其内① Accepting a lower stake: 35% → 24.99%, below the 25% blocking-rights threshold — the boundary German law drew, and we signed inside it.
  • ② 接受运营隔离：HHLA 保留 CTT 全部运营与 IT 控制——无独家使用权、无战略数据入口② Accepting operational separation: HHLA retains full operational and IT control of CTT — no exclusive use rights, no access to strategic data.
  • ③ 接受开放承诺：码头向所有航线开放、土地保持公有③ Accepting openness: the terminal serves all shipping lines; the land stays in public hands.
  • POST-PODIUM④ 接受关键基础设施定性：BSI 事后将 CTT 列为关键基础设施——我们接受德国法附加的全部条件④ Accepting the designation: BSI later listed CTT as critical infrastructure — we accept every condition German law attaches to it. At the 2022 podium, say instead: "we will accept any designation German law attaches."（本场时点该定性尚未发生，口径改为将来时承诺）
  • ⑤ 接受审查全程：投资审查机制完整走过（且结果改变了原方案）——这是机制起作用的证据，不是被绕过的证据⑤ Accepting the full review: the screening process ran end to end and changed the deal — evidence the mechanism worked, not that it was bypassed.
  • ⑥ 透明度承诺：事实以德英双语上桌（HHLA fact-check 我们逐行背书），持续接受提问⑥ Transparency: the facts are on the table in German and English (we endorse HHLA's fact-check line by line), and questions remain welcome.

Opening — the view from Shanghai

Chancellor, Senator, Ministers, ladies and gentlemen of the press — good morning. [PAUSE]
I am here on behalf of COSCO Shipping Ports. Sixty-one years ago our first ship left Shanghai. Today we run the world's largest fleet — 1,417 vessels, 141.7 million deadweight tons — and we hold stakes in 57 terminals around the world, handling 133 million TEU a year.

§2

The deal — what we bought, and what we did not

target 1:30 · words
>> [SLOW] Numbers first — credibility comes from precision.
Let me be precise about the transaction, because precision is what this debate deserves.
We originally negotiated for 35 percent of HHLA's Container Terminal Tollerort. After a full and — we accept this — difficult review, we closed at 24.99 percent. [PAUSE] That number was not our first choice. It is a deliberate legal architecture: it stays below the 25 percent threshold at which German law attaches blocking rights to a minority shareholder. We accepted it with open eyes.
So what did we buy? A financial stake in one terminal, and a long-term partnership with Germany's largest port operator. What did we not buy? [PAUSE] Control. HHLA retains sole operational management of Tollerort. No exclusive rights to the terminal. No access to strategic know-how or sensitive port data. The City of Hamburg keeps majority ownership of HHLA — and with it, the port.
For those who ask whether this is a foothold for something larger: the answer is the contract. Minority. No path to control written into it. Germany's investment-screening regime — and we respect it — retains full jurisdiction over any future change.
§3

Why Hamburg, why now — the commercial logic

target 1:00 · words
>> Commercial register — for the dealmakers in the room.
Hamburg is Europe's third-largest container port and its largest railway port. For HHLA, our partnership means a committed anchor customer and a shareholder with a balance sheet built for the long term.
For COSCO, this is one node in a network built over decades: Piraeus, where volumes rose from 1.5 to 3.7 million TEU after our investment; Zeebrugge, where we committed 300 million euros of new terminal infrastructure. We do not buy ports to own them. We invest in ports to make routes through them more reliable.
And we know the counter-narrative. Piraeus is cited as proof of ambition; we cite it as proof of competence — a terminal rescued, records set, jobs created during Greece's darkest years. [PAUSE] If our record is on trial, we are content to be judged on it.
§4

The stakeholders — one by one

target 1:30 · words — THE GRADED SECTION
>> Name each party. One line of respect, one fact. Miss none.
Every transaction of this kind is a coalition. Let me acknowledge the parties directly, because a deal that works for one party alone is a deal that fails.
To HHLA — our partner. Five thousand six hundred people, a hundred and forty years of Hanseatic logistics. You kept operational control; we bring volume and capital. Your published fact-check was correct, and we stand behind every line of it.
To the City of Hamburg — majority shareholder and host. The Free and Hanseatic City remains exactly that. Our stake does not touch the city's ownership, and it does not touch the port's governance.
To the German federal government — the ministries that scrutinized this transaction, and the Chancellor who engaged with it. The debate was hard, and it was legitimate. You defined the perimeter: 24.99 percent, no blocking rights, no strategic access. We signed inside that perimeter. That is not a loophole — that is the deal working as your law intended.
To the Federal Office for Information Security — you classified Tollerort as critical infrastructure after we signed. It is a fair question, and the answer is continued transparency: we support HHLA's commitments on operational control and data protection, and we accept whatever conditions German law attaches. [PAUSE] We invest as guests in this country. Guests follow house rules.
To our European partners — in Brussels and in every port we serve. Judge us in Athens, in Zeebrugge, in Rotterdam — and now in Hamburg.
And to the German public and the press — you are the reason we speak today. We put facts on the record, in German and in English, and we will keep doing so. Ask us hard questions. Trust in a multipolar world is not asked for — it is earned, transaction by transaction.
§5

Close — the multipolar reading

target 1:00 · words
>> Lift the register — this is the paragraph the professor grades.
Ladies and gentlemen, this transaction is a small stake in a single terminal — and, at the same time, a test case for our era. Capital now moves between systems that do not fully trust each other.
The 24.99 percent is a legal answer to a political question — how much openness can interdependence bear? Our answer is: this much, structured this carefully, under this much scrutiny — and it works. Hamburg keeps its port. COSCO secures its route. Germany gains a committed investor. [PAUSE]
That is our case. Thank you — and I look forward to your questions.

  • DO · 先答后解释：决议、数字、日期先行，一分钟后才展开背景（slide 8）Answer first, then explain — decision, number, date before background.
  • DO · 先安人心：开场先向员工与汉堡市民说话，再谈交易结构——care for people is task one（slide 6）Open by addressing employees and Hamburg's citizens before deal structure — care for people is task one.
  • DO · Bridge, don't dodge：承认问题正当（"这个问题该问"），再转向事实Acknowledge the question is legitimate, then pivot to facts.
  • DO · 一个声音：全场只引用已公布文件——内阁决定与 HHLA fact-checkOne voice: cite only published documents — the cabinet decision and HHLA's fact-check.
  • DO · 把数字变成防线：主动报出 35→24.99%、25% 否决线、1989 年总部——精确即诚意Turn numbers into your defense line: volunteer 35→24.99%, the 25% threshold, the 1989 HQ — precision reads as sincerity.
  • DO · 承认无知："我不掌握，会核实后回复"——唯一不会被打脸的回答（slide 8）"I don't have that figure; I will verify and revert" — the one answer that can never be falsified.
  • DO · 语气即信息：冷静、不设防、双手可见——the messenger is the messageCalm, unhurried, hands visible — the messenger is the message.

DON'T — 避免

  • DON'T · 猜政府意图：不替部委说话，只引用公开决定（slide 8）Never speculate about government intentions — quote public decisions only.
  • DON'T · 用"纯商业"打发安全关切：先承认关切开宗明义，再给结构答案Don't wave away security concerns with "it's just business" — acknowledge the concern, then answer with structure.
  • DON'T · 引用后见之明：2022 年 10 月的你不知道 MSC（2024）、BSI 定性、2025 复苏——引用即穿帮No hindsight: in Oct 2022 you cannot know MSC (2024), the BSI designation or the 2025 recovery — citing them breaks the scenario.
  • DON'T · 纠正提问框架：四个媒体系统四种框架——在对方框架内回应，而不是重设框架（slide 10）Don't fight the questioner's frame — four media systems, four frames; answer inside their frame.
  • DON'T · 承诺具体就业数字：只承诺结构事实——"运营与用工由 HHLA 决定，多数股权在市府"Never promise headcount numbers — promise the structural fact: "operations and staffing stay with HHLA; the majority stays with the city."
  • DON'T · 把 24.99% 说成让步：说"德国法律画的边界，我们签在线内"——同一个数字，两种叙事Don't call 24.99% a concession — "the boundary German law drew; we signed inside it." Same number, different story.
官方万能回复 · The Universal Response RESPECTFUL ON ANY QUESTION — hostile · unanswerable · off-topic 点击展开 · click to open
面对刁难、难以回答或不相关的问题，先用这段守住尊重与边界。四步：承认提问 → 划定边界 → 桥接事实 → 承诺跟进。慢速Delivery，停顿本身也是官方语言的一部分。For any hostile, hard-to-answer or off-topic question, hold respect and the boundary with this statement. Four moves: acknowledge the question, draw the boundary, bridge to facts, commit to follow up. Deliver slowly — the pauses are part of the language.

[PAUSE] 1 · RESPECT
Thank you — and let me say first: we respect the question, and we respect the concern behind it.

2 · BOUNDARY
There are matters on which I can give you facts today, and matters where I cannot — commercial confidences, or processes that are still ongoing with the authorities. On those, I will not speculate, and I would ask for your understanding: a responsible partner does not guess.

3 · BRIDGE
[SLOW] But here is what I can promise, on the record: every commitment we have made is written down — in German law, in the cabinet's conditions, and in the signed agreements. We accepted a lower stake. We accepted operational separation. And we accepted the full review. Those are not talking points; those are facts.

4 · COMMIT
We will take every question raised today seriously. Where a written follow-up is appropriate, you will receive one through the proper channels. We have been part of this city for thirty-five years — respectful dialogue is not a tactic for us. It is how we work. [PAUSE]

A · 刁难 / 带预设的问题 · Hostile or loaded questions

  • "That is a fair challenge, and I will answer it directly: …"— 然后接事实，不接情绪（acknowledge, then facts — never the tone）
  • "I understand the question behind the question — let me address that concern."— 点破预设再回应
  • "We do not shy away from hard questions. Here is the factual record: …"— 把敌意转化为背书事实的机会
  • "I would respectfully put it differently. The facts are: …"— 不接受对方框架的礼貌重述

B · 无法回答 · Cannot answer — confidential · ongoing · unknown

  • "On that specific point I am not in a position to comment — and I will not improvise. What I can tell you is: …"— 官方版 no comment：不给答案，但给出"能说的"
  • "That touches on commercially confidential matters / on a process that is still ongoing with the authorities — I hope you will understand my restraint."— 给出不能答的理由，而非沉默
  • "I don't have that figure with me today. I will not estimate at this podium — I will verify it and revert in writing."— slide 8 纪律：绝不现场估算
  • "Any answer I gave you now would be speculation — and this company does not answer by speculation."— 拒绝假设题的定版句

C · 不相关 / 跑题 · Off-topic or irrelevant

  • "That takes us beyond today's subject — but let me be clear: nothing about this transaction touches that. Today is about Hamburg."— 切断关联再拉回主题
  • "I appreciate the interest in the wider relationship — that question, however, is for the government to address. What I can speak to is this deal."— 礼貌移交，不代言政府（slide 8）
  • "Let me respectfully bring us back to what this press conference can inform: the transaction, its safeguards, and the commitments."— 主持人式收回话题
  • "We will happily follow up on the broader topic through the appropriate channels."— 软着陆收尾，不显得回避

D · 收尾 · Close with respect

  • "We take every question raised today seriously — including the uncomfortable ones. Especially those."— 化解敌意的定版句
  • "Where a written follow-up is appropriate, you will receive one through the proper channels."— 承诺可核查、可兑现
  • "We have been answering to this city for thirty-five years — respectfully, factually, and on the record."— 价值观收尾

E · 中性表述 · Neutral ground — cushion, de-politicize, land on facts

  • "China has long valued cooperation with partners around the world — our company is a product of that cooperation."— "中国一直重视与各国合作"的企业化安全版：把国家层面的表态落到"我们公司是这种合作的产物"，不代言政府
  • "Trade has connected China and Europe for decades. We see ourselves as part of that long tradition — not a break with it."— 强调延续性而非改变，回应"新进入者"叙事
  • "China has been Germany's largest trading partner for six years running. That is a fact neither side needs to be shy about."— 中性话后面立刻接可核查事实（2017–2022 连续六年），事实让中性不显得空洞
  • "We are a company, not a government. Our job is to move cargo safely and on time — and that is what we ask to be judged on."— 去政治化定版句：企业身份切割政治追问
  • "Geopolitics is for statesmen. What we bring to Hamburg is capital, cargo and a thirty-five-year track record."— 把地缘问题礼貌交还给政治家，自己落回商业事实
  • "We do not comment on politics and we do not take sides. We comply with the laws of every country we operate in."— 不选边 + 合规承诺，适用于任何地缘敏感题
  • "Economic ties have served both sides well. Our aim is to keep it that way — port by port, contract by contract."— 互利叙事的克制版：不用 win-win，用"port by port"落地
  • "We welcome oversight — from regulators, from the city, from the public. Scrutiny is not a burden; it is how trust is built."— 欢迎监督：把敌意质询重新定义为信任构建过程
  • "Open trade has been our experience in more than a hundred countries — it is the environment we know how to work in."— 以自身经验谈开放，不替任何国家立场上价值
  • "We came as customers thirty-five years ago; we stayed as neighbours. That is the spirit we bring to this table."— 中性收尾：客户→邻居的身份叙事，温度而不失分寸

E2 · 具体问题具体分析 · Case-by-case — laws, companies, contexts

  • "Each investment must be judged on its own merits — its legal framework, its terms, its partners. Blanket judgments make poor analysis and poorer policy."— 具体问题具体分析的定版句：构成要素（法律框架·条款·伙伴）点出来，再反盖"一刀切判断"
  • "Legal environments differ from country to country. That is not a caveat — it is the reason every one of our investments is structured under local law and reviewed by local authorities."— 法律环境不同：把"各国法律不同"从托词翻成"正因如此每笔投资都按当地法架构、当地审查"
  • "Every company's situation is different — ownership, governance, track record, incentives. We ask to be assessed on ours, and we will answer for ours."— 公司情况各异：股权·治理·记录·激励四要素；只请求按"我们的情况"被评估，并愿意逐项作答
  • "Context matters: a Greek port in a debt crisis, a Belgian terminal in an alliance shift, a Hamburg stake under German screening — different backgrounds, different structures, different outcomes."— 背景不同：三个案例并列成"背景论"，主动把敏感先例纳入自己的叙事框架
  • "Comparison is useful only when it is like-for-like — same law, same structure, same terms. Otherwise it is rhetoric, not analysis."— 对比须同类相比：法律·结构·条款三同才有可比性，否则是修辞不是分析
  • "We do not generalize from one case — and we would ask the same courtesy in return."— 礼貌反将：我们不以个案概括全体，也请对方如此——克制而有分寸的回敬
  • "Specific questions deserve specific answers. Generalizations are where accuracy goes to die."— 具体问题配具体答案：泛化是准确性的坟墓——记者会最硬的合法性话语
  • "Where the honest answer is 'it depends', we will say so — and then tell you exactly what it depends on."— 最高级的官话：承认"看情况"，再讲清"取决于什么"——诚实版 it depends，比硬编答案可信得多
使用纪律 — ①绝不说 "no comment"（永远用"不在评论位置 + 原因 + 能说的"替代）；②绝不说 "off the record"；③不猜数字、不代言政府意图（slide 8）；④官话的功能是守住尊重与边界，事实桥接才拿分；⑤中性表述的主语永远用公司/港口/贸易——"China values cooperation"要说成"our company is a product of that cooperation"，不替国家表态；⑥不用 win-win、shared future 等口号直译——观感是宣传不是回答；⑦每句中性话后面尽快接一个可核查事实（六年第一大伙伴 · 35 年 · 57 码头 · 100+ 国家）。Ground rules: never "no comment" (always: not in a position + reason + what you can say); never "off the record"; no invented numbers; no speaking for the government. The boilerplate holds the line — the bridge to facts wins the point.
▸ 他国失败案例 · 应答框架 OTHER COUNTRIES' FAILURES — how to answer any "X country failed" question（点击展开）
  • 框架一 · 法律各国不同：每个法域一部自己的法——比利时是比利时的、希腊是希腊的、汉堡是德国法+联邦审查+共决制。"different countries, different laws, different structures" 先立住，再谈个案。Framework 1 — laws differ: every jurisdiction has its own law; this deal sits inside German screening, cabinet conditions and co-determination. Establish that first, then discuss the case.
  • 框架二 · 换位与自省：不否认、不甩锅——先给失败的诚实解读（货随联盟走、危机中的控股难局），再邀请对方把问题也问向自己："这个教训对我们意味着什么？"Framework 2 — reflect, don't deflect: concede the honest reading of the failure (cargo follows alliances; a controlling stake in a crisis is hard), then invite the mirror question: what does that lesson mean for us here?
  • 样本翻面：57 个码头持股、多数合作续约——单一失败不是均值；用大样本回应个案。Flip the sample: 57 terminals, most partnerships renewed — one failure is not the average.
  • 落回汉堡事实：所有框架最终落回"汉堡按汉堡的事实评判"——24.99%、运营隔离、开放承诺。Land on Hamburg: every framework ends at "Hamburg will be judged on Hamburg's facts" — 24.99%, operational separation, open access.

NEW · XJ 01Xinjiang — the supply-chain question
"Reports link Xinjiang to forced labour in supply chains. COSCO is state-owned. What is your answer on Xinjiang?"
"We hear the concern, and we take it seriously. EXT 01 · Ministriesscreening logic
Why 24.99% and not 25? Was this structured to dodge our review?
We negotiated 35% in good faith. The government defined the perimeter; we accepted it. 24.99% is not below review — the transaction was reviewed in full under the AWV. It is below the statutory blocking-rights threshold, which is a choice Germany wrote into law. Any future change of control triggers review again.

NEW · XJ 04Transparency & fairness in operations
"Once you are inside, how do we know operations stay transparent and fair — that CTT doesn't quietly favour COSCO's own ships or disadvantage competitors?"
"Because we cannot favour anyone — that is the architecture. HHLA retains operational control: slot allocation, terminal systems and customer data stay with the operator and its German governance. The terminal serves all shipping lines on equal, published terms — that commitment is part of the arrangement, not a courtesy. Our own vessels are customers of the terminal under the same terms as every other carrier, and MESL — the port's independent tariff supervisor — can review exactly that question. So when competitors ask 'who checks', the answer is: the operator we do not control, German governance, and an independent regulator — and our own ships live under the same rules as everyone else's."
STRAT · EN Pre-empt the "backdoor favouritism" fear with structure, not assurances: (1) HHLA keeps operational control — slot allocation, systems, data; (2) equal published terms for all lines, written into the arrangement; (3) COSCO's own ships are customers on the same terms as competitors; (4) independent tariff supervision (MESL) can audit exactly this. Close on symmetry — "our ships live under the same rules".
战术 · 中 用结构而非保证来预防"开后门偏袒"疑虑：①HHLA 保留运营控制（泊位分配·系统·数据）；②所有航线同等公开条款，写进安排本身；③COSCO 自己的船也按同等条款做客户；④独立费率监管（MESL）恰可审查此事。落点对称性——"我们的船和别人适用同样规则"。

战术 · 中 拒绝空洞保证——商业目标支撑就业 + 程序正确：先劳资会、后公告（德国法定顺序）。

NEW · GR 01Piraeus impact — good & bad
"You always cite Piraeus. We are from Piraeus — we lived your investment. What did it actually bring us, good and bad?"
"An honest answer has both sides. The good: volumes rose from 1.5 to 3.7 million TEU, records were set year after year, jobs were held through Greece's darkest years, and the port became a Mediterranean hub again. The bad: a difficult start — labor conditions and safety complaints that were real, environmental friction with the local community, and a feeling among some Greeks that decisions had moved to Shanghai. We learned from that: local management, local hiring, and listening earlier. That is the lesson we bring to Hamburg."
STRAT · EN Own the negatives before listing the positives — with any host community, credibility starts with naming the costs first. And note the difference: at Piraeus we were majority owner and operator; in Hamburg HHLA operates and German law governs.
战术 · 中 先认坏处再列好处——对任何东道社区，先说出代价才有公信力；并点明结构差异：Piraeus 是控股运营，汉堡是少数股权+德国法管辖。
NEW · GR 02labor record follow-up
"Greek dockers called early conditions at Piraeus 'sweatshop-like'. Why should German workers believe it will be different here?"
"Because the structure is different — and so is the law. At Piraeus we were the majority owner and operator; here HHLA retains operational control, and German labor law, co-determination and the works council apply in full. At Piraeus conditions did improve substantially after the early frictions — pay agreements, safety programs, local hiring. In Hamburg we neither set employment terms nor decide the workforce: those decisions belong to HHLA and its representatives."
STRAT · EN Don't dispute the history — dispute the transfer: different ownership, different operator, different legal system. Concede-and-contrast beats deny.
战术 · 中 不争辩历史——争辩"可迁移性"：股权不同、运营方不同、法律不同。"承认再对比"胜过否认。
NEW · GR 03post-acquisition plans
"So what exactly will COSCO do with its 24.99%? What are your concrete plans for Tollerort after closing?"
"Three things, all inside the governance we agreed. First, volumes: we anchor our China services at Tollerort and grow them with the market. Second, support: backing HHLA's investment program, especially rail connections that make Hamburg stronger against Rotterdam and Antwerp. Third, discipline: no operational role, no data access, and we will review cargo concentration together with HHLA. What we will not do: set employment terms, redirect the terminal's strategy, or use it against other customers."
STRAT · EN Plans must be concrete but conditional — three do's inside the agreed governance, one explicit not-do list. Specificity inside restraint is the whole message.
战术 · 中 计划要具体但守界——三件"会做"（都框在已定治理内）+ 一串明确"不做"。克制中的具体性就是全部信息。
NEW · GR 04cargo cannibalization
"Will you shift cargo from Piraeus to Hamburg — does one port's gain become the other's loss?"
"No — they serve different trades. Piraeus is the Mediterranean gateway and a transshipment hub for Asia routes; Hamburg is the North European gateway for Central European cargo and Europe's largest rail port. The network grows with total demand — in 2025 both ports grew. We invest in nodes because customers need reliability at both ends, not because one steals from the other."
STRAT · EN Kill the zero-sum frame with geography: different gateways, different trades, both grew in 2025. Facts end conspiracy.
战术 · 中 用地理杀死零和叙事：不同门户、不同航线、2025 两港双增——事实终结阴谋论。
NEW · PY 02Piraeus — the record abroad as a warning?
"Greek dockers called early conditions at Piraeus 'sweatshop-like', and local communities complained for years. Isn't that record exactly why countries should be careful with COSCO?"
"We respect that question — it deserves a straight answer, not a deflection. Start with the framework: Piraeus was a controlling stake under Greek law, in the middle of a debt crisis; Hamburg is a minority stake under German law, with HHLA operating and the works council in place — different countries, different laws, different structures. On the record itself: the early years saw real friction — labor conditions, safety complaints, community tension. Conditions then improved substantially: pay agreements, safety programs, local hiring, and volumes that rose from 1.5 to 3.7 million TEU. And we learned — local management, local hiring, listening earlier. So I would put the question back, respectfully: the lesson of Piraeus is not 'be careful with COSCO'; it is 'set the framework right, and the investment delivers'. That is exactly what Germany has done here — and why we accepted it."
STRAT · EN Concede the record straight (no deflection), then split frameworks (Greek crisis + controlling stake vs German law + minority + co-determination), then reframe the lesson: the warning is not about COSCO, it is about frameworks — and Germany's framework is the answer. Close by tying it back to the 24.99% acceptance.
战术 · 中 先直面记录不回避；再切割框架（希腊危机+控股 vs 德国法+少数股权+共决制）；最后重述教训——比港的警告不是"小心 COSCO"，而是"把框架设对，投资就能兑现"；德国恰恰做到了，这正是我们接受 24.99% 的原因。
EU & policy watchers欧盟与政策观察者 — dependence, pattern, the Russia analogy §
CORE 02dependence / Russia analogy
"Is Germany repeating its mistake of becoming dependent on Russian energy?"
"The concern about dependence is legitimate. Limits on ownership address control, but dependence on a major customer requires separate monitoring. We would support reviewing cargo concentration and maintaining a broad customer base."
STRAT · EN Don't fight the analogy — split control from dependence, and offer the monitoring you support.
战术 · 中 不否认类比的正当性——把"控制"与"依赖"拆成两题，主动支持货量集中度监测：批评转成我们也在推动的机制。
EXT 04 · EU-mindedpattern charge
Isn't Hamburg part of a pattern — a dozen European ports with Chinese stakes?
We hold stakes in terminals across Europe and we are proud of each: Piraeus, Zeebrugge, Antwerp, Rotterdam, Bilbao, Valencia, Naples. Each is a separate commercial contract under national law and EU screening rules. A network of contracts is not a strategy of control; it is a strategy of service.
NEW · XJ 03ESG — what will you actually do?
"ESG commitments are easy to announce and hard to keep. Concretely — what will COSCO's ESG practice look like in Hamburg, and who checks it?"
"Let me make it concrete, because you are right — announcements are cheap. Environment: we support HHLA's existing climate program and its published targets; as a shareholder we align with them, we do not set separate ones from afar. Social: employment terms are set by HHLA under German co-determination — the works council is the check, not us. Governance: German law, the cabinet's conditions, and this deal's own architecture — 24.99 percent, no operational control — are the checks. And reporting: a listed company reports in Germany under German transparency law; as a minority shareholder we are inside that regime, not outside it. So: HHLA's targets, the works council, German law, and public reporting — four checks, none of them ours, all of them effective."
STRAT · EN The strongest ESG answer for a minority shareholder: E/S/G each mapped to the institution that already owns it — HHLA's climate targets (E), works council and co-determination (S), German law and the deal architecture (G), plus public reporting as the verification layer. Land on "four checks, none of them ours, all of them effective" — restraint converted into accountability. Never invent new COSCO-specific targets at the podium.
战术 · 中 少数股东的最强 ESG 答案：E/S/G 各自映射到已有的制度主人——HHLA 气候目标（E）、劳资会与共决制（S）、德国法与交易架构（G）、公开报告作验证层。落点"四道检查，没有一道是我们的，全部有效"——把克制转化为问责。绝不在台上新造 COSCO 自己的指标。

"We cannot predict decisions by either government. We would comply with applicable requirements and work with HHLA on continuity planning. That planning should test disrupted cargo flows and regulatory changes."
STRAT · EN Never predict governments — answer with your obligations and your stress-tested continuity plan.

EXT 05 · Mediathe Scholz question
The Chancellor was Hamburg's mayor. Did political weight tilt this deal?
The process ran through Germany's investment-screening machinery, with multiple ministries involved and the outcome changed from our original proposal — that is evidence of the process working, not of it being bypassed. Heads of government routinely engage on strategic transactions; the decision that matters was taken in the ministries, per law.
EXT 06 · Media hard follow-upSOE trust
You are state-owned. Beijing could direct you. Why should anyone trust "no control"?
Two answers. First, legal: the contract bars control — no blocking minority, no operational role, no data access — and German law re-engages at any change. Second, structural: our economics here are throughput economics. Using a container terminal against its owner's interest destroys the value we paid for. Trust is not a press release; it is the record — invite scrutiny of Piraeus, Zeebrugge and now Hamburg.
NEW · PY 01Zeebrugge — the port authority walked away
"In Belgium, the Zeebrugge port authority publicly blamed your CSP terminal for falling cargo volumes and chose not to renew cooperation. If your own host partner in Belgium calls the investment a failure, why should Hamburg expect a different outcome?"
"Every port, and every legal framework, is different — and that is precisely the point. Belgium's framework is Belgium's; this transaction is governed by German law, screened by the German government, and conditioned by the cabinet's own decision. On Zeebrugge: we hold a majority in CSP and we remain its largest customer by far; cargo follows trade lanes and alliances, not ownership — that is the honest reading of what happened there. And we hold ourselves to the same test we invite here: judge the operation by its published numbers. But I would also gently turn the question around: the fair comparison is not one terminal in one country — it is how we behaved in each of the fifty-seven ports where we are a shareholder. In most of them, the partnership was renewed. Hamburg will be judged on Hamburg's facts, under Hamburg's laws — and on that record we are confident."
STRAT · EN Three moves: (1) laws and frameworks differ by country — Belgium is not Germany, this deal sits inside German screening and conditions; (2) give the honest reading of the failure itself (alliances reshaped trade lanes; volumes follow cargo, not ownership) without blaming the host; (3) turn the sample size around — 57 ports, most partnerships renewed — then land on "Hamburg will be judged on Hamburg's facts".
战术 · 中 三步：①各国法律框架不同——比利时不是德国，本交易在德国审查与条件之内；②对该失败给出诚实解读（联盟改航线、货随联盟走，不甩锅东道方）；③把样本翻过来——57 个码头多数续约——最后落回"汉堡按汉堡的事实被评判"。

How does this case map to the course frameworks?
